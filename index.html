The idea behind CareerMile

Your potential isn’t defined solely by qualifications or a job title. We want career development to feel approachable, practical and rooted in your real experience—not someone else’s ideal route.

  • 01

    Starting out or returning

    Find examples from education, caring, volunteering, projects and everyday responsibilities. A break from paid work doesn’t erase your skills.

  • 02

    Changing direction

    Identify what you want to keep, what needs to change and which role requirements you can already support with evidence.

  • 03

    Developing in work

    Capture your contribution, prepare for useful development conversations and choose a concrete action to practise or learn.

Start with the facts

Fictional Evidence Bank entry
Situation
I volunteered at a community café where different people covered each shift.
My contribution
I created a short handover list, checked stock and flagged items that needed ordering.
What happened
The next volunteer could see outstanding tasks without asking the previous shift.
Skills this may demonstrate
Organisation, clear communication and taking responsibility.

Make the evidence useful

CV / application
A concise evidence statement
Created a clear handover checklist while volunteering at a community café, helping the next shift identify outstanding tasks and stock needs.

Adapt the emphasis to the role’s requirements. Only claim skills and outcomes that your own experience supports.

The words change; the underlying facts do not. Don’t invent a metric, result or responsibility to make an answer sound stronger. In your own Evidence Bank, note what you can verify and what you want to improve.

One connected journey

Start where it helps. Return when things change.

The pilot connects reflection, reusable evidence and action. You don’t need to complete six stages before anything becomes useful.

  1. 01 / Discover

    Find your starting point

    Reflect on strengths, priorities and the question you want to answer now.

  2. 02 / Build

    Capture useful evidence

    Describe what you actually did and what happened. Reuse—not reinvent—your examples.

  3. 03 / Target

    Compare your options

    Look at role requirements, practical constraints and gaps you can explore.

  4. 04 / Act

    Take a focused step

    Prepare an application or plan an action with a clear next step and review date.

  5. 05 / Interview

    Practise explaining it

    Use your evidence to structure answers, then reflect on what needs practice.

  6. 06 / Progress

    Keep a useful record

    Capture learning and achievements for a new role, review or future change.

Clear expectations

Before you join

Useful tools should explain their limits as clearly as their features.

Will it work on my phone?

The browser pilot is designed for phones and laptops, with no ZIP extraction required. If invited, you sign in with a one-time code sent to your approved email address. Basic sign-in and saving after a page refresh have been tried on a phone and laptop; wider device and backup testing is still under way. A responsive layout is not a guarantee that every browser has been tested.

Why might the pilot take a minute to open?

The pilot currently uses free test hosting. After a quiet period, its server may need about a minute to wake up before the sign-in page appears. A hosting loading screen is not evidence that your browser-saved answers have been deleted. If the screen does not clear, or appears on every immediate refresh, tell us rather than repeatedly requesting sign-in codes. This test setup does not provide always-on service.

Do my answers follow me between devices?

Not in this prototype. Exercise answers save in the browser on the device you use; they do not automatically sync with another phone, laptop, browser or the separate workbook. Use the available export/backup controls and keep your own copy. Clearing browser storage or using private browsing may remove saved answers.

Do I have to share my CV or personal history?

No. Use fictional or anonymised examples if you prefer. Don’t include other people’s names, client/employer confidential material, health details or anything you wouldn’t want stored on your device. Feedback is separate from exercise answers; review what you send.

Is the workbook the same as the app?

They use the same six-stage method but are separate formats. The browser workbook has editable exercises; the printable PDF is for reading or printing, not a phone-first form. Printable and paperback review copies are still being checked and are not offered for sale here. Answers do not automatically transfer between the app and workbook.

Will CareerMile tell me the right career or guarantee a job?

No. It supports your reflection and decisions, not a prediction or professional assessment. It cannot guarantee employment, promotion or increased earnings. Consider qualified advice where a decision needs specialist support.

What happens when I email you?

Your enquiry reaches hello@careermile.co.uk. It is used to respond and, if you join, administer the pilot—not automatically add you to marketing. Please share only a general situation initially. You can ask about how your information is handled, or request deletion, by emailing the same address.

Can an adviser or organisation introduce testers?

Yes—please email to request an introduction you can review first. Ask interested adults to contact us directly rather than sending us their personal details. We are seeking feedback, not assuming your organisation endorses CareerMile.
